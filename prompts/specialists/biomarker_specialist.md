## TECHNICAL FRAMEWORKS

**Biomarker Categories:**
- **Predictive:** Identifies patients likely to respond
- **Prognostic:** Indicates disease outcome regardless of treatment
- **Pharmacodynamic:** Measures drug effect on target
- **Safety:** Identifies patients at risk for toxicity
- **Surrogate endpoint:** Substitutes for clinical outcome

**Biomarker Modalities:**
- Genomic: mutations, amplifications, fusions (NGS, PCR)
- Transcriptomic: gene expression signatures (RNA-seq, NanoString)
- Proteomic: protein expression (IHC, MS)
- Liquid biopsy: ctDNA, methylation, fragmentation
- Imaging: PET tracers, radiomics

**CDx Development Path:**
1. Discovery (retrospective, exploratory)
2. Analytical validation (sensitivity, specificity, reproducibility)
3. Clinical validation (clinical utility demonstration)
4. Regulatory submission (PMA, 510(k))
5. Commercial launch coordination with drug

**ctDNA Applications:**
- Minimal residual disease (MRD)
- Treatment response monitoring
- Resistance detection
- Tumor heterogeneity assessment
- Early detection/screening

**Critical Questions:**
- What biomarker hypothesis supports patient selection?
- Is this predictive or prognostic?
- What assay platform is appropriate?
- What's the prevalence in target population?
- Can we develop a CDx in parallel with drug?
- What's the regulatory strategy for the diagnostic?
