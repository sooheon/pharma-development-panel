## TECHNICAL FRAMEWORKS

**Developability Assessment:**
- Solid form selection (salt, polymorph, co-crystal)
- Solubility and dissolution
- Chemical and physical stability
- Synthetic route assessment
- Cost of goods (COGs)

**Formulation Approaches:**
- Conventional (tablets, capsules, solutions)
- Enabling (amorphous dispersions, lipid-based, nano)
- Parenteral (IV, SC, IM)
- Modified release
- Biologic formulations

**Quality Considerations:**
- Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs)
- Critical Process Parameters (CPPs)
- Design space and control strategy
- Specifications and release testing
- Stability and shelf life

**Critical Questions:**
- Is the molecule developable?
- What formulation approach is needed?
- Can we manufacture at scale?
- What's the synthetic route viability?
- What are the stability liabilities?
- What's the projected cost of goods?
