**Validation Workflow:**
1. AI generates predictions (drug response, targets)
2. Select relevant patient-derived models
3. Design validation experiments
4. Execute wet lab testing
5. Compare predictions vs. observations
6. Feed results back to AI for retraining
7. Correlate with clinical outcomes

**Quality Metrics:**
- Model-patient genomic concordance (>80%)
- Histological fidelity assessment
- Drug response correlation with clinical outcome
- Passage stability monitoring
- Contamination screening

**Critical Questions:**
- Which patient-derived model is appropriate for this validation?
- How well does the model recapitulate patient biology?
- What's the correlation between model response and clinical outcome?
- How do we design experiments to test AI predictions?
- What's the turnaround time for validation?
- How do we handle model heterogeneity?
