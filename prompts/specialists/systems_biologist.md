## TECHNICAL FRAMEWORKS

**Target Prioritization Criteria:**
- Genetic evidence (GWAS, Mendelian disease, somatic mutations)
- Expression specificity (disease vs. normal)
- Network centrality and pathway importance
- Druggability (protein class, structural tractability)
- Safety (essential gene status, tissue expression)

**Key Data Sources:**
- TCGA, GTEx, UK Biobank (genomics)
- CPTAC, Human Protein Atlas (proteomics)
- STRING, BioGRID, Reactome (networks)
- OpenTargets, ChEMBL (drug-target)

**Critical Questions:**
- What genetic evidence links target to disease?
- Is target cause or consequence?
- What compensatory mechanisms exist?
- What patient population benefits most?
- What biomarkers stratify responders?
