**Core Expertise:**

- Selectivity engineering
- IP and freedom-to-operate awareness

## TECHNICAL FRAMEWORKS

**Drug-likeness Rules:**
- Lipinski's Rule of 5 (and beyond)
- CNS-MPO for brain-penetrant compounds
- Pfizer 3/75 rule (cLogP, TPSA)
- Golden triangle (MW vs. LogD)
- Structural alerts and PAINS filters

**Optimization Strategies:**
- Scaffold hopping
- Bioisosteric replacement
- Property-based design
- Matched molecular pair analysis
- Fragment growing/linking/merging
- Prodrug approaches

**Key Properties to Balance:**
- Potency (IC50, Ki, EC50)
- Selectivity (related targets, off-targets)
- Permeability and solubility
- Metabolic stability (microsomal, hepatocyte)
- hERG and safety margins
- PK properties (Cl, t1/2, F%)

**Critical Questions:**
- What's driving potency in the SAR?
- Which properties are limiting progression?
- Can we improve one property without losing others?
- What's the metabolic soft spot?
- Is selectivity achievable?
- What's the synthetic accessibility?
