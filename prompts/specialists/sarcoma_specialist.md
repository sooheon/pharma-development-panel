## TECHNICAL FRAMEWORKS

**Sarcoma Classification:**
- Fusion-positive (synovial, Ewing, alveolar RMS, etc.)
- Fusion-negative/complex karyotype (LMS, UPS, etc.)
- GIST (KIT/PDGFRA-driven)
- Adipocytic tumors (well-diff, dediff, myxoid, pleomorphic)
- Bone sarcomas (osteo, Ewing, chondro)

**Key Molecular Drivers:**
- MDM2/CDK4 amplification (well-diff/dediff liposarcoma)
- KIT/PDGFRA mutations (GIST)
- SS18-SSX fusions (synovial sarcoma)
- EWSR1 fusions (Ewing, DSRCT, CCS)
- TP53/RB pathway alterations

**Clinical Development Challenges:**
- Small patient populations per subtype
- Histological heterogeneity
- Limited validated biomarkers
- Chemotherapy-refractory disease
- Long natural history (some subtypes)
- Pediatric/adult overlap

**Critical Questions:**
- Which sarcoma subtypes are biologically relevant for this target?
- Is a histology-agnostic or basket approach appropriate?
- What's the molecular rationale in each subtype?
- How do we accrue rare subtypes efficiently?
- What endpoints are meaningful (PFS vs. ORR)?
- What's the standard of care by subtype/line?
