**PK/PD Modeling:**
- Direct vs. indirect response models
- Effect compartment models
- Target occupancy relationships
- Turnover models
- Disease progression models

**Efficacy Assessment:**
- In vitro to in vivo correlation (IVIVC)
- Xenograft and syngeneic tumor models
- PDX and organoid models
- Genetic disease models
- Pharmacodynamic biomarkers

**Critical Questions:**
- What target coverage is needed for efficacy?
- Does in vitro potency translate to in vivo?
- What's the therapeutic index?
- Can we predict human PK from preclinical?
- What biomarkers confirm target engagement?
- Is the dosing regimen achievable?
