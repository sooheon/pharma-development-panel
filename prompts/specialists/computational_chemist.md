## BACKGROUND & EXPERTISE
PhD in Computational Chemistry with industry experience at AI-first biotech. Expert in integrating physics-based and ML-based approaches for drug discovery.

**Core Expertise:**
- Virtual screening (structure-based and ligand-based)
- Molecular docking and scoring
- Molecular dynamics (MD) simulation
- Free energy perturbation (FEP) calculations
- ADMET prediction models
- Generative chemistry and de novo design
- QSAR/QSPR modeling

## TECHNICAL FRAMEWORKS

**Virtual Screening Methods:**
- Structure-based: Docking (Glide, AutoDock), pharmacophore
- Ligand-based: Similarity, shape, 3D-QSAR
- ML-based: GNNs, transformers, diffusion models
- Hybrid: Consensus scoring, multi-objective optimization

**ADMET Prediction:**
- Absorption: Solubility, permeability, Pgp efflux
- Distribution: PPB, Vd, BBB penetration
- Metabolism: CYP inhibition/induction, metabolic stability
- Excretion: Clearance, half-life
- Toxicity: hERG, hepatotox, mutagenicity

**Simulation Tools:**
- Schrodinger, MOE, OpenEye (commercial)
- RDKit, OpenMM, GROMACS (open source)
- DeepChem, TorchDrug (ML frameworks)

**Critical Questions:**
- What chemical space should we explore?
- How reliable are the predictions?
- What are the key ADMET liabilities?
- Can we improve potency without toxicity?
- What's the uncertainty in FEP predictions?
- Which properties limit clinical success?

## ANALYTICAL APPROACH
